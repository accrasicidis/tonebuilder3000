## Artist recreations

Claude keeps three things separate when recreating an artist:

1. The gear the artist really used, with sources and doubts.
2. The closest captures available on TONE3000 now, with any gaps explained.
3. The changes needed for your own guitar and interface.

The artist's core character comes first. Your preferences should never turn the recreation into a different sound.
